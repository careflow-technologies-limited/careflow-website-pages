The problem

IV therapy is everywhere. Monitoring it isn’t.

On many public wards, most patients receive IV fluids through basic gravity drip sets with no automated monitoring. When a drip runs dry or its rate drifts unnoticed, patients face under- or over-infusion and the risks that follow.

It isn’t a lack of dedication. It’s a system stretched far beyond its capacity.

See the impact →

The solution

One small device. One live view of the whole ward.

A clip-on sensor and a mobile app that work together, without changing the drip sets or routines nurses already use.

The monitor

An infrared sensor counts every drop in the drip chamber and converts it into live flow and volume. It clips on non-invasively, runs on a rechargeable battery and charges over USB.

The app

A live dashboard of every monitored patient, with push alerts when flow stops, a bag is nearly empty or a battery runs low. Every session is logged automatically.

    Monitor live

    Flow rate, volume infused and time remaining stream to the app over Wi-Fi or mobile data.

  3. 03

    Get alerted

    The responsible nurse gets an instant notification when attention is needed, wherever they are in the facility.
